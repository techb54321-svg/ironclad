Questions

Is Ironclad free right now?

Yes. Everything, including Ironclad alerts, is free while Ironclad is in beta. Free stays free after that.

What happens when Pro opens?

If you joined, you'll get one email saying it's open. Nothing changes for anyone without plenty of notice first.

Does Ironclad hold my deposit?

No. Your referee holds your deposit and sends forfeits where you agreed. Ironclad never touches your stake money; Pro would only be the subscription.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, once paid plans exist. You'd drop back to Free and keep your whole history.

I'm a personal trainer. How would Coach work?
